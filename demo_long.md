# Q3 2025 Performance Report

## Revenue Overview

Total revenue for Q3 2025 reached $4.2M, representing a 23% increase year-over-year.
The SaaS segment continues to be our growth engine, contributing 68% of total revenue.

- SaaS recurring revenue: $2.85M (+31% YoY)
- Professional services: $890K (+12% YoY)
- Licensing: $460K (-8% YoY)

## Key Metrics

Our customer base expanded significantly this quarter:

- Active customers: 1,247 (up from 1,089 in Q2)
- Net Revenue Retention: 118%
- Monthly churn rate: 1.2%
- Average contract value: $42,500

## Product Development

The engineering team shipped three major releases this quarter.

### Platform 3.0 Launch

The new platform architecture reduces page load times by 45% and supports
real-time collaboration for up to 50 concurrent users.

### AI Features Beta

We launched our AI assistant in beta with 200 early adopters:

- 87% user satisfaction score
- Average 3.2 hours saved per week per user
- 12 enterprise customers in pilot program
