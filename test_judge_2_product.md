## Go-to-Market Timeline

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Jan-Mar)
- Complete beta testing with 50 enterprise customers
- Finalize pricing and packaging
- Train sales team on new product features
- Develop marketing collateral and demo environments

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Apr-Jun)
- Launch to existing customer base
- Gather initial feedback and iterate
- Begin PR campaign
- Host three regional launch events

### Phase 3: Full Launch (Jul-Sep)
- National advertising campaign
- Partner channel activation
- Conference presence at 5 major events
- Aggressive outbound sales push
