# Team Offsite Agenda

## Purpose

Align the product and engineering teams on H2 priorities and build team cohesion.

## Schedule

The offsite runs from June 15-17 at the Mountain View campus.

- Day 1: Strategy presentations and breakout sessions
- Day 2: Hackathon and innovation showcase
- Day 3: Team building and action planning

## Key Topics

### Product Roadmap

We will review the product roadmap for H2 and prioritize features based on customer feedback and market analysis.

### Engineering Excellence

Focus areas include reducing technical debt, improving CI/CD pipelines, and adopting new testing frameworks.

### Customer Insights

Review NPS data and customer interview findings. Current NPS is 72, up from 65 last quarter.

## Logistics

- Location: Building 42, Conference Center
- Capacity: 50 people
- Catering: Breakfast and lunch provided
- Remote participants: Zoom bridge available

## Expected Outcomes

1. Finalized H2 roadmap with owner assignments
2. Three hackathon projects selected for incubation
3. Updated team OKRs for Q3 and Q4
4. Cross-team collaboration agreements
