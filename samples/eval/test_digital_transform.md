# Digital Transformation Roadmap 2026

## Executive Overview

Our 3-year digital transformation initiative enters its critical second year. This roadmap outlines the strategic priorities, investment areas, and expected outcomes for 2026. Total planned investment: $45 million across technology modernization, workforce upskilling, and process reengineering.

The transformation addresses three core imperatives:
- Customer experience enhancement (40% of investment)
- Operational efficiency gains (35% of investment)
- Innovation and growth enablement (25% of investment)

## Strategic Initiatives

### Initiative 1: Customer Experience Platform

**Objective:** Unified omnichannel customer engagement

**Scope:**
- Mobile app redesign with AI-powered features
- Chatbot deployment across all channels
- Real-time personalization engine
- Customer 360 data platform

**Timeline:** Q1-Q3 2026

**Investment:** $12.5M

**Expected Outcomes:**
- NPS improvement: +25 points
- Digital channel adoption: 75% (from 45%)
- First contact resolution: 85%
- Customer effort score: 2.1 (from 3.8)

### Initiative 2: Intelligent Automation

**Objective:** Automate 60% of repetitive manual processes

**Target Processes:**
1. Invoice processing (2,400 hours/month saved)
2. Customer onboarding (65% faster)
3. Claims adjudication (80% touchless)
4. Report generation (95% automated)
5. Data entry and validation (90% automated)

**Technology Stack:**
- RPA platform: UiPath Enterprise
- Intelligent document processing: ABBYY
- Process mining: Celonis
- Workflow orchestration: ServiceNow

**Investment:** $8.5M

### Initiative 3: Cloud Migration

**Migration Strategy Comparison:**

| Approach | Risk Level | Time | Cost | Benefit |
|----------|------------|------|------|---------|
| Lift and Shift | Low | 6 months | $3.2M | Quick migration |
| Replatform | Medium | 12 months | $5.8M | Better optimization |
| Refactor | High | 18 months | $9.5M | Maximum efficiency |
| Replace (SaaS) | Medium | 9 months | $4.2M | Modern capabilities |

**Selected Approach:** Hybrid (40% Replatform, 35% SaaS, 25% Refactor)

**Target Architecture:**
- Primary cloud: AWS (70%)
- Secondary cloud: Azure (20%)
- Hybrid/Edge: On-premise (10%)

**Investment:** $14M
