# Q4 2025 Financial Performance Report

## Executive Summary

TechVista Corporation delivered exceptional results in Q4 2025, exceeding analyst expectations across all key metrics. Revenue grew 34% year-over-year to $2.8 billion, driven by strong enterprise adoption and international expansion. Net income reached $485 million, up 42% from Q4 2024.

Key highlights include:
- Cloud services revenue: $1.2B (+58% YoY)
- Enterprise customers: 12,400 (+28% YoY)
- Operating margin: 24.3% (vs 21.1% Q4 2024)
- Free cash flow: $620M (+35% YoY)
