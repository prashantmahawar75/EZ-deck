# Sales Enablement Automation Review

## Executive Overview

The sales-enablement automation pilot improved win rate from 24% to 31% and reduced average lead response time from 9 hours to 25 minutes. Reps saved 6.5 hours per week on prep work, and CRM field completion improved from 61% to 92%.

## Before vs After Sales Workflow

- Lead response time: 9 hours vs 25 minutes
- Discovery prep time: 45 minutes vs 10 minutes
- Proposal turnaround: 3 days vs same day
- CRM field completion: 61% vs 92%
- Weekly manager coaching coverage: 40% vs 85%

## 5-Step Launch Process

- Audit existing playbooks and win-loss notes in week 1
- Train the retrieval model on approved battlecards in week 2
- Pilot AI-assisted call prep with 12 account executives in week 3
- Roll out proposal drafting after legal review reaches 95% accuracy
- Expand to all regions with weekly governance reviews in week 6

## Performance Gains

- Win rate improved from 24% to 31%
- Average sales cycle fell from 54 days to 42 days
- Reps saved 6.5 hours per week on prep work
- Manager coaching participation rose from 40% to 85%

## Risks and Guardrails

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
